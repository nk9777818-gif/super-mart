7-Day Return & Refund Policy

Eligible products may be requested for return within 7 days of delivery. The item should be unused, in its original packaging, and include its accessories and proof of purchase.

How to request a return

Contact SUPER MART support with your order ID, product details and reason for return. Our team will review the request and confirm whether the item is eligible.

Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed after the returned product is received and inspected. For Cash on Delivery orders, refund method and timing will be confirmed by our support team.

Exceptions

Items marked non-returnable, damaged due to misuse, incomplete, or returned without original packaging may not qualify, subject to applicable consumer law.
